# Quiet Garden artwork

- Generated on 2026-10-22 with the built-in `image_gen` tool.
- Production asset: `quiet-garden.webp` (1536 × 1024). The original generated PNG was encoded as WebP without changing its composition.
- Decorative hero for Zen Garden and its full daily reading. Illustrated botanical guardians and pointer ripples are rendered separately. Subtle CSS filters vary the scene by season and time of day.
- This illustration deliberately uses a forest-and-moonlight palette in both app theme modes.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background illustration for a small premium Zen Garden wellness UI hero, wide 3:2 landscape. Create a refined Japanese garden at blue-green twilight, painted in soft layered gouache and atmospheric sumi-e ink on subtly grained matte paper. Quiet winding stepping stones, gently raked sand, moss-covered low rocks, delicate ferns, a tiny stone lantern with a faint warm glow on the far right, distant layered misty mountain silhouettes and a very subtle pale moon. Sophisticated editorial illustration, organic brush texture and believable depth, restrained detail, luxurious calm. Palette: deep evergreen #13231c, muted forest green, dusty sage, charcoal, small touches warm ivory and aged gold, low saturation. Composition is designed for UI text overlay: keep the upper-left and middle-left 45 percent dark, soft and uncluttered with mist and subtle hillside texture; put the main scenic details in the right and lower areas. Leave a small empty mossy stone platform at about x72%, y76% for a separate interactive potted botanical guardian rendered by the app; do not put a tree or plant on that platform. Full bleed image. Soft ambient light with enough shadow detail to read at 370px wide, no high contrast bright sky. No words, no letters, no UI, no border, no logo, no watermark, no people. Avoid flat cartoon shapes, anime, glossy 3D, neon colors, purple gradients, and noisy dense foliage.

## Botanical illustrations

- A second image was generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool as a transparent 5 × 2 botanical atlas (1983 × 793).
- The ten complete plants were sliced with transparent padding and encoded as individual 320 × 320 WebP assets in `plants/`. Alpha is preserved. No generative or creative edits were made during slicing.
- Atlas order: sprout, blossom, bamboo, aloe, lotus; pampas, bonsai, fungi, ginkgo, willow. Existing SVG art remains available as a fallback for unlisted species.

Output a 5:2 wide aspect ratio, ideally 2560x1024 pixels; strict regular grid with invisible boundaries. Genuine transparent background, no white background and no checkerboard drawn into the image. All specimens painted in the same refined matte gouache and delicate Japanese botanical ink style: organic brushwork, subtle natural depth, softly illuminated desaturated sage and evergreen leaves, restrained dusty rose and antique ivory accents, dark earthy ceramic planters. Editorial illustration, elegant miniature bonsai collection, no cartoon or glossy3D or neon. Every plant clearly distinct and complete including its pot, no overlap between cells. In exact left-to-right order: TOP ROW cell1 a tiny fresh two-leaf green seedling in a shallow charcoal ceramic pot; cell2 delicate cherry blossom bonsai with dusty blush pink flowers and brown branches in a shallow dark pot; cell3 a small upright cluster of thin bamboo stems and fine sage leaves in a charcoal ceramic pot; cell4 a golden aloe rosette with olive green pointed succulent leaves subtly edged ochre, dark low ceramic planter; cell5 a single pale pink lotus flower over rounded dusky green lily pads in a shallow dark water bowl.
